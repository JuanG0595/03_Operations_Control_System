# Welcome to my project portfolio called Operations Control System

## Introduction

This project was created using fictional data and aims to simulate a controlled operations-administrative environment in which I seek to establish certain parameters, processes, and other elements that will demonstrate proficiency in the use of relevant tools and knowledge in this field.

### Business Case

Northbridge Business Solutions — fictional construction and field-support company that manufactures prefabricated houses and provides personnel, technical knowledge and operational support to construction projects carried out by other companies.

Inside the company, the administrative team is experiencing an increased workload that has affected the organization, tracking and timely processing of routine operational requests.

This manifests as:
- A backlog of requests
- Records that need updating
- Documents that need organizing
- Tasks without follow-up
- Meetings that need coordination
- Information that is not centralized

### Business Objective

To design and implement a practical Operations Control System that provides visibility, organization and traceability across routine administrative activities.

### Operational Requirements

The company needs a structured system to organize, track, process and monitor administrative requests, documentation, records, schedules and follow-up activities.
